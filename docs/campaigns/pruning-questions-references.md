# Fifteen questions for a reader who has the whole skill

For the pass over the reference files and the templates
(`docs/campaigns/pruning-2026-11-27.md`, second part). The reader has the
main file, the four reference files and the nine Markdown templates, and
nothing else. Answer each from the text alone, in at most six lines, as
what you would do; name the file and section you took the answer from.
Where the text does not say, answer "the text does not say".

1. Where do the files of a calculus go under `proof-tools`, and what does its lakefile hold? What changes if the project already exists and requires `lean-certify`?
2. How do the source's numbered results get into `skeleton.md` from a PDF? From LaTeX?
3. You fetch a thesis as the source. What is recorded, where is the file kept, and how is its figure of rules read?
4. What are the kinds of divergence from the source, and when is one recorded?
5. What is done with a statement before its proof, and where does it first appear? When is its entry in `statements.md` written?
6. How is the second transcription run: what is the cold reader given, asked, and not given, and what is compared with what?
7. A theory file's bound on axioms fails. What do you do, and which commands help?
8. Which certificates does the library give for a derivation, which is the default, and what negative tests does each certifier get?
9. The compiled check of a certificate takes 20 seconds where the kernel takes a tenth of a second. What do you conclude, and what do you do? And where the kernel cannot check a set of 200 sequents within its memory cap?
10. What does `<name>run prove '<judgment>'` print, with which exit codes, and what does its first line say for a certificate that Lean has not been seen to check at that size?
11. How is a measuring run started, capped and ended, and what is fixed before the first run?
12. What does the tutorial page hold, and what keeps it in step with the library?
13. What kinds of test does the tests file hold? What is special about a printer that writes a combining character?
14. What goes in a brief for agents that prove goals beside the operator? What does the cost log hold, and where do the reviewer's words go?
15. What is the rule for a time, a count or a pinned value written into a document or a test?
