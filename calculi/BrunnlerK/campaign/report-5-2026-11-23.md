## Retrospective

**What the campaign adopted.** Brünnler's nested sequent system K, from
the thesis text (arXiv:1004.1845 v1, §2.1): formulas in negation normal
form; nested sequents as lists; the family `Fam` for K and K with cut,
every rule stated for a variable conclusion, a path and the node at
that path; the family `FamO` for K°; a rule table with fourteen
divergences, signed.

**What it proved.** Eight of the ten goals of the plan, and with them
every result the source proves about system K at `X = ∅` except two
about its own search: soundness; invertibility; the admissible rules,
with the depth; completeness; the finite model property; decidability;
cut elimination, as admissibility. Beyond the source: exchange, which
its multisets assume; soundness of K with cut; the general axiom as a
function. In  lines of theory files, of  lines of Lean by
hand in all.

**What runs.** A search, proved to end and to be right, that returns a
proof or a finite tree; it reduces in the kernel, so validity is
decided by `decide +kernel`. Compiled, it is `ksearch`. Certifiers for
trees of moves and for countermodels; a corpus of 22 sequents with
every fact checked. An explorer: `kmove`, scripts, walks, tactics of
the calculus, a tutorial.

**What is OPEN.** Nothing that was a goal. Not attempted: cut
elimination as the procedure of §2.1.4; K°; the systems with `d`, `t`,
`b`, `4`, `5`; any statement of complexity.

**What it cost.** One session of about eleven and a half hours by the
clock from the command to this report, most of it waiting on the
reviewer between stages: the stages themselves took, by the cost log,
34 minutes (Survey), 22 (Adopt), about 60 (Prove), 11 (Run) and a few
(Explore). Requests, tokens and cost are not known to the operator.
